The center adopts a director-responsibility model and establishes an academic advisory committee. With academic guidance, team collaboration, project-driven research, and open sharing mechanisms, it builds an operating system that connects internal university teams, external partners, and industry collaborations. The center is planned around three teams: multi-source fusion positioning, urban spatio-temporal intelligence, and embodied intelligent sensing.
