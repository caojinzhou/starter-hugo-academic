## Research Environment

- The center is equipped with deep learning servers, urban big data training platforms, urban intelligence development kits, and high-performance workstations.
- We maintain large-scale urban built-environment and mobility datasets, including POIs, buildings, smart cards, ride-hailing GPS trajectories, bike-sharing data, mobile phone positioning, Tencent location data, geotagged social media, nighttime lights, and street-view imagery.
- Students are supported to attend domestic and international conferences and to grow through real research projects, paper writing, system development, and teamwork.
- We collaborate with universities and groups including Wuhan University, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, HKUST, University of Washington, UCL, and Shenzhen University.

## Master Students

- Background in computer science, data science, GIS, remote sensing, urban science, or related fields.
- Solid programming, data analysis, machine learning, or deep learning skills.
- Ability to analyze research problems independently, learn quickly, and work well in a team.

## Senior Undergraduate Students

- Experience with Python, R, JavaScript, C/C++, or strong motivation to learn quickly.
- Interest in algorithms, data structures, machine learning, deep learning, Geo-AI, or urban computing.
- Willingness to read English literature and work steadily on challenging problems.
- Experience in frontend/backend development, visualization, Deck.gl, WebGIS, or system development is a plus.
